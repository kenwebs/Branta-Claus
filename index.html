The Purpose

When Did Giving Become So Much About Buying?

Christmas can be full of shopping lists, expensive gifts, and expectations. We measure generosity by the price tag, and lose sight of the profound simplicity of the season.

But the spirit of giving asks us to look beyond what we purchase. It challenges us to remember the people who need our care, our time, and our tangible support. It's time to reclaim generosity.
